Privacy Policy for ONE WRONG MOVE

Effective date: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Guru Hi-Tech Engineering ("we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the ONE WRONG MOVE mobile game.

ONE WRONG MOVE does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, messages, or other personal information in order to play the game.

The current version of ONE WRONG MOVE does not intentionally collect personal or sensitive device information for our own purposes. The current version of ONE WRONG MOVE does not intentionally collect personal information for advertising or analytics purposes on behalf of Guru Hi-Tech Engineering.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal information collected by the current version of ONE WRONG MOVE with third parties because the current version does not intentionally collect personal information for our own purposes.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Because the current version does not require an account and does not intentionally transmit personal information to us, we do not maintain a server-side personal account database for the game.

Locally stored game progress and settings can be removed by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's storage through Android device settings, subject to Android's system behavior and any device backup features.

6. Children's Privacy

ONE WRONG MOVE is a general-audience puzzle game and is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The current version does not require personal information to play.

7. Data Security

We design the current version of ONE WRONG MOVE so that personal information is not required or intentionally collected by the app. Any information stored locally is handled by the app and Android's application storage mechanisms.
